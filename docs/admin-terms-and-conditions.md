# RunNation Global Admin Terms and Conditions

Version: `2026-05-23`

## Mission

- RunNation exists to connect runners, clubs, organizers, families, writers, and community leaders through safe events, trusted records, inclusive clubs, responsible communication, and inspiring stories.
- Admin access is given to protect that mission and to help the platform serve members fairly, accurately, and professionally.

## Values

- Integrity: Admin decisions must be honest, evidence-based, and free from favoritism, retaliation, or personal advantage.
- Safety: Admins must protect users from abuse, unsafe content, misleading information, and careless handling of personal data.
- Service: Admin access must be used to support runners, clubs, events, magazine contributors, and the wider RunNation community.
- Accountability: Admin actions may be logged and reviewed, and misuse of access may lead to removal of the role or further action.
## Copyright Disclaimer

- Authors, submitters, and photo contributors are responsible for ensuring that every article, image, caption, attachment, external link, or other magazine material they submit is original, licensed, credited, or otherwise lawful to publish.
- RunNation may review, edit, reject, remove, or publish submitted magazine material, but copyright infringement liability remains with the originating author, submitter, or contributor who provided the material.
- Admins and editors should reject or remove magazine content when there is a credible concern that the article, picture, or supporting material may infringe another person's rights.
## Role Terms

- Global Admin is responsible for role approval, including approving or rejecting service roles such as Event Organizers, Club Coordinators, Special Club Coordinators, Magazine Editor, Chat Room Administrator, and approved columnist roles.
- Global Admin is responsible for account data health, including monitoring accounts, reviewing profile integrity issues, identifying broken account links, and approving justified repairs.
- Global Admin is responsible for audit accountability, including reviewing admin actions, investigating misuse, and keeping audit information within approved RunNation leadership channels.
- Global Admin is responsible for milestones, orders, stock, payments, archive records, reports, suggestions, and ratings where these records support platform management, service quality, fulfilment, recognition, or data care.
- Global Admin is responsible for event oversight across countries and clubs, including event approval, participant limits, payment instructions, event readiness, and linked magazine content where required.
- Global Admin is responsible for club administration, enrollments, club and organiser requests, treadmill approvals, activity uploads, other source runs, and WhatsApp group oversight where platform-wide intervention is needed.
- Global Admin is responsible for magazine oversight, including articles, event stories, community submissions, pictorials, columnist work, and publication decisions.
- Global Admin is responsible for chat safety, including reviewing abuse reports, deleting harmful posts or comments, and approving or applying username-based chat suspensions where evidence supports action.

## Acceptance

- By accepting, you agree to carry out Global Admin responsibilities only within your assigned scope and only for legitimate RunNation work.

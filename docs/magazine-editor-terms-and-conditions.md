# RunNation Magazine Editor Terms and Conditions

Version: `2026-05-25`

## Mission

- RunNation exists to connect runners, clubs, organizers, families, writers, and community leaders through safe events, trusted records, inclusive clubs, responsible communication, and inspiring stories.
- Admin access is given to protect that mission and to help the platform serve members fairly, accurately, and professionally.

## Values

- Integrity: Admin decisions must be honest, evidence-based, and free from favoritism, retaliation, or personal advantage.
- Safety: Admins must protect users from abuse, unsafe content, misleading information, and careless handling of personal data.
- Service: Admin access must be used to support runners, clubs, events, magazine contributors, and the wider RunNation community.
- Accountability: Admin actions may be logged and reviewed, and misuse of access may lead to removal of the role or further action.
## Copyright Disclaimer

- Authors, submitters, and photo contributors are responsible for ensuring that every article, image, caption, attachment, external link, or other magazine material they submit is original, licensed, credited, or otherwise lawful to publish.
- RunNation may review, edit, reject, remove, or publish submitted magazine material, but copyright infringement liability remains with the originating author, submitter, or contributor who provided the material.
- Admins and editors should reject or remove magazine content when there is a credible concern that the article, picture, or supporting material may infringe another person's rights.
## Role Terms

- Magazine Editor is responsible for The Running Post magazine workflow, including article submissions, event stories, community articles, journalist pieces, fitness coach content, empowerment coach content, columnist work, pictorials, captions, photos, and publication status.
- Magazine Editor is responsible for reviewing submissions for grammar, clarity, tone, accuracy, consistency, copyright risk, and publication readiness before they appear in The Running Post.
- Magazine Editor may approve, reject, return, or delete magazine content according to RunNation editorial standards and may set publication-ready magazine items where the workflow allows.
- Magazine Editor may handle breaking news and time-sensitive updates by quickly preparing, updating, and publishing suitable stories when needed.
- Magazine Editor may coordinate sponsored or advertiser-supported content, but must maintain editorial integrity, transparency, and reader trust.
- Magazine Editor is responsible for leading the editorial vision by shaping the magazine's identity, tone, themes, content calendar, and long-term growth strategy.
- Magazine Editor may write the News column, but the editor's own News articles must be approved by Global Admin before publication.

## Acceptance

- By accepting, you agree to carry out Magazine Editor responsibilities only within your assigned scope and only for legitimate RunNation work.

# Junior Runners Club Coordinator Terms and Conditions

Version: `2026-05-25`

## Mission

RunNation exists to connect runners, clubs, organizers, families, writers, and community leaders through safe events, trusted records, inclusive communities, responsible communication, and inspiring stories.

The role of a Junior Runners Club Coordinator is to support young runners, encourage participation in healthy activities, promote club growth, and represent the values of RunNation within the club community.

## Core Values

### Integrity

You must carry out your responsibilities honestly, fairly, and in the best interests of club members. Decisions should be based on facts and established guidelines, not personal relationships or preferences.

### Safety

You are expected to prioritize the wellbeing of junior members and to help maintain a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment both online and during club activities.

### Service

Your role exists to support club members, parents, guardians, volunteers, and the wider RunNation community through positive leadership and responsible administration.

### Accountability

Actions performed using coordinator privileges may be logged and reviewed. Failure to meet the responsibilities of the role may result in suspension or removal of coordinator privileges.

## Coordinator Responsibilities

### Club Activities and Events

- Plan, organize, and promote club runs, walks, training sessions, and other approved club activities.
- Ensure that club activities are conducted in a safe and responsible manner.
- Communicate event details accurately and in a timely manner to club members.

### Activity Verification

- Review and approve eligible activity records submitted from external sources, including smart watches, fitness trackers, running applications, and treadmill workouts.
- Reject or request clarification for records that appear inaccurate, incomplete, duplicated, or inconsistent with RunNation guidelines.

### Club Communications

- Manage and moderate official club communication channels, including WhatsApp groups or other approved platforms.
- Encourage respectful and constructive discussions.
- Remove inappropriate content and address behaviour that may negatively affect club members or the reputation of the club.

### Club Stories and Recognition

- Write and publish club-related articles, event summaries, member achievements, and feature stories highlighting positive contributions within the club.
- Ensure that published content is accurate, respectful, and suitable for the RunNation community.

### Member Support

- Welcome new members and help them understand club activities and expectations.
- Promote participation, sportsmanship, inclusion, and a positive club culture.

## Copyright and Content Responsibility

When publishing articles, photographs, videos, or other media on behalf of the club, you must take reasonable steps to ensure that the material is original, properly licensed, appropriately credited, or otherwise lawful to publish.

Where there is a credible concern that content may infringe another person's copyright or rights, you must refrain from publishing the material and report the matter for review.

## Scope of Authority

Your coordinator privileges are limited to your assigned club and approved RunNation activities. You must not use coordinator access for personal gain, political activity, harassment, commercial promotion unrelated to RunNation, or any activity that may harm members or the reputation of RunNation.

## Acceptance

By accepting this role, you agree to carry out the responsibilities of a Junior Runners Club Coordinator professionally, ethically, and within the limits of the authority granted to you by RunNation.
